Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Field Hockey is a game made by Andrew Tennison ("we"). This page tells you what data the game collects, why, where it is kept, and how to have it deleted. We keep it short and we collect as little as we can.

The short version

  • You do not need to give a name or email to play.
  • We record how you play (game results and the controls you pressed) to make the game better.
  • Usage analytics are sent only if you say yes.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • Data is stored in the European Union.

What we collect

DataWhat it isWhy
Account id A random id made at first launch. It is not your name or email. To keep your club and your play history together.
Your club The club and player names you choose, shirt colour, points, upgrades and record. To save your progress and restore it on a new install.
Play sessions For each match or practice run: mode, team size, teams, start and end time, play time, score and result. Also a log of the controls you pressed, so we can replay the game exactly. To find bugs and to balance the game (for example, if a mode is too hard).
Usage analytics (only with your consent) Screens you open, games you start and finish, purchases in the in-game shop (game points only, no money), app and game version, control type (touch or keyboard). To learn which parts of the game people use and where they stop.
Device and version Device type, app version and game version. If the game cannot reach our servers (for example, sign-in or a session upload fails), and you allowed analytics, we send a short error report: which step failed, the error code, and the first 200 characters of the error message. We remove ids, email addresses and IP addresses from the message before it is sent. To find bugs that happen only on some devices or versions.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising id. The game has no ads and no real-money purchases.

Optional sign-in

Later, you will be able to save your progress with Sign in with Apple or Google. If you do, we store the email address and name that Apple or Google gives us, only to link your account. Apple lets you hide your real email. If you do not sign in, we have no email for you.

Where it is stored, and who processes it

  • Supabase (servers in the EU): your account id, club, session summaries and play logs.
  • PostHog (EU cloud): usage analytics, only if you consent.
  • Apple: if you test with TestFlight, Apple shares your tester email and any feedback you send with us, under Apple's own privacy policy.

These services process data for us and may not use it for their own purposes.

Your consent

At first launch the game asks if you agree to usage analytics. You can play if you say no. You can change your mind at any time in the game's settings.

How long we keep it

  • Account id, club and session summaries: until 12 months after your last play session, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Replay logs (the controls you pressed): 12 months from the session.
  • Usage analytics (PostHog): 1 year.

If we stop the game, we delete all data.

Your rights, and deletion

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or its deletion. Email support@tennisons.com. To find your data, we may ask for details such as your device and the date you started to play. We reply, and do a deletion, within 30 days.

If you are in the UK or EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not ask for a name, email or age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (under 16 in some EU countries) beyond the anonymous game data above. If you think your child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top. If a change is important, we tell you in the game.

Contact
