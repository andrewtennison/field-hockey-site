Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Send an email to [CONTACT EMAIL]. We read every message and try to reply within a few days.

What to put in your email

  • What happened, and what you expected.
  • Your device (for example, iPhone 14) and its iOS version.
  • The app version, if you know it (TestFlight and the App Store show it).
  • A screenshot, if you can.

Testers (TestFlight)

If you test the game with TestFlight, you can also send feedback from the TestFlight app: take a screenshot in the game, then tap Share Beta Feedback.

Your data

To get a copy of your data, or to have it deleted, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. The privacy policy tells you what we keep and why.
